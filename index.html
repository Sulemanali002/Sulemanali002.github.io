GLOF project: what I did and found

The question

Can where, when, lake type and trigger of a glacier lake outburst flood predict whether it had any recorded impact?

The data

HMAGLOFDB, a database of historical GLOFs. 137 of its 703 events are in Gilgit-Baltistan, and 61 had a recorded impact. "No impact recorded" does not mean no impact happened.

The method

Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, Decision Tree and Logistic Regression, tested with leave-one-out cross-validation against a 55.5% baseline.

The result

Gradient Boosting scored best at 70.1%. Tree models beat the baseline by about 14 points, and differences between them are within noise.

What I learned

Location and year carry most of the signal, so the model mostly learns where and when events were documented, not physical hazard. A single train/test split ranged from 50% to 89%, so I report cross-validated results.

Next steps

Add physical data such as glacier area, lake area, slope and temperature, and test on a larger regional dataset.
